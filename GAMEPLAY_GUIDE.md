# Clash Game - Gameplay Guide

## 🎮 Getting Started

### Main Menu
When you launch the game, you'll see three options:

1. **New Game**: Start a fresh game with initial resources
2. **Continue**: Load your saved progress
3. **Exit**: Close the game

## 🏰 Base Management

### Your Starting Base
When you start a new game, you'll have:
- 1000 Gold
- 1000 Elixir
- 50 Gems
- A Town Hall (level 1)
- A Gold Mine
- An Elixir Collector

### Building Your Base

#### Opening the Build Menu
1. Tap the **"Build"** button at the top
2. Select a building type from the menu
3. Tap on the grid to place it

#### Available Buildings

**Town Hall** (Free first one)
- Your main building
- Protects your base
- Must be defended in battle
- HP: 1000 (increases with level)

**Gold Mine** (200 Gold)
- Generates gold over time
- Production: 10 gold/second at level 1
- Size: 2x2 grid cells

**Elixir Collector** (200 Gold)
- Generates elixir over time
- Production: 10 elixir/second at level 1
- Size: 2x2 grid cells

**Storage** (300 Gold)
- Increases resource capacity
- Adds 5000 storage at level 1
- Size: 2x2 grid cells

**Barracks** (500 Gold)
- Allows troop training
- Must have at least one to train troops
- Size: 3x3 grid cells

**Wall** (50 Gold)
- Basic defense structure
- Blocks enemy movement
- Size: 1x1 grid cell
- HP: 100 at level 1

**Archer Tower** (400 Gold)
- Defensive structure that attacks
- Damage: 10 at level 1
- Range: 5 tiles
- Size: 2x2 grid cells

### Building Placement Rules
- Buildings must fit on the grid (40x22)
- Cannot overlap with existing buildings
- Choose strategic positions for defenses
- Place resource buildings near storage

### Upgrading Buildings
1. Tap a building to select it
2. If eligible, an upgrade button appears
3. Pay the upgrade cost
4. Wait for construction time
5. Each building can reach level 5

## 💰 Resource Management

### Resource Types

**Gold** 🪙
- Used for building and upgrading
- Generated by Gold Mines
- Stored in your base (max capacity)
- Gained from victories

**Elixir** ⚗️
- Used for training troops
- Generated by Elixir Collectors
- Stored in your base (max capacity)
- Gained from victories

**Gems** 💎
- Premium currency
- Currently for display (future updates)
- Starting amount: 50

### Collecting Resources
Resources are collected automatically every 10 seconds from:
- Gold Mines → Adds to your gold
- Elixir Collectors → Adds to your elixir

The game also auto-saves every 30 seconds!

## ⚔️ Training Troops

### Accessing the Barracks
1. Tap the **"Barracks"** button
2. View available troops
3. Select a troop to train

### Available Troops

**Barbarian** (50 Elixir)
- Melee fighter
- HP: 100
- Damage: 15
- Speed: Fast
- Range: Close combat only
- Best for: Frontline attacks

**Archer** (80 Elixir)
- Ranged attacker
- HP: 60
- Damage: 12
- Speed: Medium
- Range: 5 tiles
- Best for: Supporting from behind

**Giant** (200 Elixir)
- Tank unit
- HP: 500
- Damage: 25
- Speed: Slow
- Range: Close combat only
- Best for: Absorbing damage

### Training Process
1. Click on a troop type
2. Pay the elixir cost
3. Wait 5 seconds for training
4. Troop added to your army
5. Maximum capacity: 20 troops

## 🎯 Combat System

### Starting a Battle
1. Train troops in the Barracks
2. Tap the **"Attack"** button
3. Enter the battle screen

### Battle Mechanics

#### Deploying Troops
- Tap troop buttons at bottom
- Click on the map to place troops
- Each troop type has different strengths
- Deploy strategically

#### Troop AI
Troops automatically:
- Find nearest building
- Move towards it
- Attack when in range
- Switch targets when building destroyed

#### Enemy Base
The enemy base includes:
- Town Hall (main target)
- Gold Mines and Elixir Collectors
- Archer Towers (will attack your troops)
- Walls (block movement)

### Victory Conditions
You win if you:
1. **Destroy the Town Hall**, OR
2. **Destroy 50%+ of all buildings**

### Battle Rewards
- Victory: +500 Gold, +300 Elixir
- Defeat: No rewards

### Battle Strategy Tips
1. Deploy Giants first to absorb damage
2. Follow with Barbarians and Archers
3. Target defenses (Archer Towers) first
4. Spread troops to avoid area damage
5. Save some troops for cleanup

### Retreating
- Tap **"Retreat"** button to end battle
- Counts as defeat (no rewards)
- Use if battle going poorly

## 💾 Saving Your Progress

### Auto-Save
The game automatically saves:
- Every 30 seconds
- When you pause/exit
- After major actions

### Manual Save
- Tap the **"Save"** button anytime
- Recommended before closing game

### What Gets Saved
- All building positions and levels
- Current resources
- Building HP
- Construction progress
- Your base layout

### Loading
- Tap "Continue" at main menu
- All progress restored
- Resume where you left off

## 📊 Progress Tracking

### Resources Display

- Current Gold amount
- Current Elixir amount
- Current Gems amount

### Building Information
When viewing buildings:
- Level indicator (Lv1, Lv2, etc.)
- HP bar (if damaged)
- Construction timer (if upgrading)

### Battle Stats
During battle, see:
- Troops remaining
- Destruction percentage
- Real-time combat

## 🎯 Strategy Tips

### Early Game (Start)
1. Build 2-3 Gold Mines immediately
2. Build 2-3 Elixir Collectors
3. Build Storage to increase capacity
4. Place walls around Town Hall
5. Build 1-2 Archer Towers

### Mid Game (Growing)
1. Upgrade Town Hall to level 2
2. Upgrade all resource buildings
3. Build Barracks
4. Create more defenses
5. Start training troops

### Late Game (Strong)
1. Max out all buildings to level 5
2. Create layered defense with walls
3. Multiple Archer Towers
4. Train diverse army
5. Attack for resources

### Defense Layout
- Put Town Hall in center
- Surround with walls
- Place Archer Towers at corners
- Protect resource buildings
- Create compartments with walls

### Attack Strategy
- Scout enemy base layout
- Identify weak points
- Deploy troops smartly
- Target defenses first
- Overwhelm with numbers

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: How do I get more resources?**
A: Build more Gold Mines and Elixir Collectors, or win battles.

**Q: Why can't I place a building?**
A: Check if you have enough gold and if the space is clear.

**Q: How do I heal damaged buildings?**
A: Buildings heal automatically over time (future feature).

**Q: Can I move buildings after placing?**
A: Not yet - plan placement carefully!

**Q: What happens if I lose a battle?**
A: You lose the troops used but can train more.

**Q: How many buildings can I have?**
A: Limited by grid space (40x22 tiles).

**Q: Do resources keep generating when game is closed?**
A: Currently only when game is open (offline generation planned).
